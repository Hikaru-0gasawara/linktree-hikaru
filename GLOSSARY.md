# Hikaru's card deck

Hikaru Ogasawara's link page, presented as a deck of cards: his own card, his project cards, then printed Magic-style cards.

## Cards

**Deck**:
The ordered set of cards on the page, browsed one card at a time.
_Avoid_: Stack, carousel

**Hikaru's card**:
The first card: photo, name, role, focus, languages, base, the About brief, soft skills and the four contact buttons. It is live text, not an image.
_Avoid_: Business card (that name belongs to the Magic business cards), profile card

**Project card**:
A card for one of Hikaru's projects, with art, type line, brief, stack, highlights, status and buttons to the project.
_Avoid_: Portfolio card (Portfolio is one project card among them)

**Magic business card**:
A printed Magic-style card that presents Hikaru, with a "how to summon" back carrying his contacts. Shown as an image.
_Avoid_: Cartão de visita (when writing in English), MTG card

**Single Magic card**:
A Magic-style card shown on its own that is not a business card, such as the Kōgen cards.

**Card group**:
Three Magic-style cards sharing one place in the deck, opened as a fan.
_Avoid_: Trio, pack, set

**Soft skills**:
The short personal traits shown as chips on Hikaru's card, limited to two rows.
_Avoid_: Skills (ambiguous with technical skills), tags

## Modes

**TCG mode**:
The way of browsing where each card is a 3D object you turn, flip and leaf through.
_Avoid_: Card mode, 3D mode

**Plain mode**:
The scrolling way of browsing, one card per gesture, the default on touch screens.
_Avoid_: Scroll mode, mobile mode

**Magic frame**:
The Magic: The Gathering–style card front in the vintage newspaper showcase style (masthead with the mana cost, headline bar, art window, type line, text box, collector strip) that replaces the look of Hikaru's card and of every project card, present and future, in TCG mode. The card keeps all of its content.
_Avoid_: Skin, theme

**Color identity**:
The Magic colors (white, blue, black, red, green, or colorless artifact) a card's frame is dressed in, chosen freely to suit the card.
_Avoid_: Theme color, palette

**Arrival animation**:
The motion a card plays in plain mode each time it comes on screen, from either direction.
_Avoid_: Entrance (only the first load), assembly (the project cards' kind of arrival animation)
